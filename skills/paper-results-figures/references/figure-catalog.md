# Figure Catalog

Read this catalog before opening a category specification. Match the scientific question and observation structure to an implemented category. A requested category takes precedence when suitable for the supplied data. Once category/layout and palette are established, its reusable renderer applies the documented visual defaults without a separate cosmetic brief. Use the new input's scientific mappings, ranges and selections rather than inheriting synthetic example choices.

There are currently **six implemented categories**. `multi-comparison` is accepted as an alias of `mutl-comparison`; `trend-comparison` is an alias of the requested `trend-comparsion` spelling. Additional categories will be added iteratively. See the [synthetic gallery](../examples/README.md) for a reproducible preview of every category.

Metric-colored dual-axis legends use a rectangular bar key and a line key matching the plotted mean icon, with metric names only. The reusable layout applies this convention without a separate cosmetic request; see [the multiple-metric specification](multi-metric-comparison.md#choose-the-layout).

## Selection boundaries

Use paired scatter for matched measurements of the same metric on comparable scales. Use multiple-model comparison for categorical method summaries or distributions, and the multiple-metric category for combined views. Summary means cannot reconstruct box plots, violins or paired differences. Continuous relationships and ordered category profiles use trend-comparsion; survival and other unsupported questions require a suitable new category. Regression alone does not establish causation or independent sampling.

Inspect format and meaning separately: a wide table does not itself require a bar chart, and numeric method codes do not make a continuous covariate. Check method/metric count, observation unit, pairing, units and established domains, available distributions versus summaries, and the scientific question before choosing the category. Use [input preparation](data-preparation.md) when a clear mapping/reshape is needed.

Honor the delivery mode: a specified suitable category, one recommended figure, or a small set of eligible candidates. For two matched methods and a question about agreement or per-observation improvement, prefer paired scatter; marginal histograms/KDE can additionally show the two score distributions. For several methods and a distribution question, use raw box/violin displays; summary-only comparisons use bars or requested mean lines. For multiple metrics, choose separate views or an appropriate combined layout according to the request. Parallel horizontal bars and stacked vertical bars share one method order and labels; they can use larger internal values or omit displayed SD. Shared-axis mean lines require identical established units and domains, not merely similar observed ranges. Otherwise use distinct labeled axes or facets; do not silently normalize. Clarify scientific ambiguity before rendering; adaptable cosmetic settings do not require a questionnaire. Do not add unsupported candidates merely to reach a requested maximum count.

Grouped model-colored bar/box/violin clusters for multiple metrics extend `multi-metric-comparison` through `combo_layout: grouped-distributions`, requiring identical established units/domains. An explicit empty numeric interval may be omitted for raw boxes using `axis_break`; the break must preserve observations, box/whisker support and shown uncertainty, and be visibly disclosed. These are options of existing categories. Radar requires scientifically justified scaling and does not establish a performance ranking from polygon area.
